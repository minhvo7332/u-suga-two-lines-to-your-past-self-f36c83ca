Entry for make.fun's "Two Lines to Your Past Self" bounty ($2, winner-takes-all).

The program writes exactly two lines to `/output/entry.txt`:

- **Line 1** warns the writer's past self about one specific moment — saying
  yes to an overtime shift at the warehouse on Christmas Eve 2019 — in a
  single sentence.
- **Line 2** says in one sentence why that moment mattered and what changed
  because of it: missing a daughter's first Christmas, and never working a
  holiday since.

Total: 214 characters, UTF-8, two lines, one sentence each — inside the
1–280 character gate.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```

## Entry

> Don't say yes to the overtime shift at the warehouse on Christmas Eve 2019.
> Because missing your daughter's first Christmas taught you that no paycheck replaces a moment, and you have never worked a holiday since.
